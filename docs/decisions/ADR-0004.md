# ADR-0004

# Angola Fiscal Compliance Architecture

**Status:** Accepted

**Date:** 2026-09-12

**Decision Makers:**

- Helder Gonçalves

---

# 1. Context

The project's vision was reframed (Project Charter §1–2, PRD §1–3) from a portfolio platform into a **commercial ERP for the Angolan market**, in which statutory fiscal compliance is a primary, non-negotiable requirement. The [Angola Fiscal Compliance discovery](../compliance/Angola-Fiscal-Compliance-Discovery.md) established the compliance areas; the owner (an Angolan ERP consultant) then supplied concrete technical facts about the AGT regime.

The current architecture does not reflect any of this. The Software Architecture Document (§7) defines no fiscal bounded context — invoices sit informally inside "Sales". The Data and Domain Models carry no fiscal notion: `TaxCode` is only code/name/rate, `Customer`/`Supplier` have no NIF or fiscal address, `Currency` has no exchange rate, and there is no `Company` entity at all.

Known AGT requirements the architecture must satisfy:

- Invoicing software must be **certifiable by the AGT**.
- **SAF-T (AO)** XML export, validating against the official XSD (`AuditFile` → `Header`, `MasterFiles`, `GeneralLedgerEntries`, `SourceDocuments`/`SalesInvoices`/`Payments`/`MovementOfGoods`/`WorkingDocuments`).
- **Electronic Invoice API** (asynchronous): `POST …/sigt/fe/v1/registarFactura` (homologation `sifphml.minfin.gov.ao`, production `sifp.minfin.gov.ao`), Basic Auth, returns a `requestID`, status queried later.
- **Two distinct signature mechanisms** (see §7): the SAF-T/printed-document **hash chain**, and the e-invoice API **JWS RS256** signatures (`jwsSoftwareSignature`, `jwsDocumentSignature`, `jwsSignature`) over the canonical JSON object.
- **IVA** (standard 14%, plus specific rates/regimes) with mandatory **exemption reason codes and legal grounds** when not liable.
- Legal document **series and gap-free numbering**; compliant invoice PDF content; auditability and record integrity.

A decision is needed on how to structure the platform for this **before** the transactional modules (EP-004 Inventory, EP-006 Sales, EP-007 Finance) are built, because these requirements impose fields and invariants on the core data model and are expensive to retrofit.

---

# 2. Problem Statement

Decide the architectural approach that makes ERP Intelligence Platform compliant with Angolan fiscal law and AGT-certifiable, and decide whether that work must precede the transactional modules.

---

# 3. Considered Options

## Option 1 — Add fiscal fields to Sales/Finance as they are built

Advantages: no upfront work; modules progress immediately.

Disadvantages: SAF-T is effectively a contract over the *whole* data model (master files + source documents); bolting fiscal concerns on per-module produces inconsistency, duplicated tax logic, and a near-certain rewrite. Certification and signature/numbering are cross-cutting and cannot be retrofitted cleanly.

## Option 2 — A dedicated FiscalCompliance bounded context, fiscal-data-model-first (chosen)

Advantages: one authoritative home for fiscal rules, documents, numbering, signatures, SAF-T and AGT integration; master data extended once, consistently; Sales/Finance built *on top* of a compliant core; certification-ready by construction.

Disadvantages: delays the transactional modules by a foundation phase; more design upfront.

## Option 3 — Integrate a third-party Angolan fiscal engine

Advantages: potentially faster certification.

Disadvantages: external dependency, licensing cost, loss of control over a core differentiator; still requires our data model to carry the fiscal fields. Not aligned with building a proprietary, profitable product.

---

# 4. Decision

Adopt **Option 2**. Introduce a **`FiscalCompliance` bounded context** and make fiscal modelling a **data-model-first foundation that precedes the transactional modules**.

Key decisions:

1. **New bounded context `FiscalCompliance`**, owning: `CompanyTaxProfile`, `Establishment`, `FiscalDocumentType`, `FiscalSeries`, `FiscalNumbering`, `TaxRegime`, `TaxCode` (moved/extended), `TaxExemptionReason`, `FiscalDocument`, `FiscalDocumentLine`, `FiscalDocumentTax`, `FiscalSignature`/document hash, `SaftExport`, `ElectronicInvoiceSubmission`, `AgtIntegrationLog`.
2. **Two separate signature mechanisms**, never merged:
   - **`DocumentHash` (SAF-T / printed):** `InvoiceDate;SystemEntryDate;InvoiceNo;GrossTotal;PreviousHash`, per series/type, `PreviousHash` empty on the first document; `GrossTotal` formatted with two decimals, `.` decimal separator, no thousands separator. Computed **offline at document finalisation**; printed on the PDF and emitted in SAF-T.
   - **`ElectronicInvoiceSubmission` JWS (AGT API):** RS256 JWS over the canonical JSON, online and **asynchronous**, independent of the SAF-T hash.
3. **Asynchronous AGT submission via an Outbox + state machine.** Document finalisation MUST NOT block on the AGT's synchronous response; `registarFactura` returns a `requestID`, submission state (`Pending→Submitted→Accepted/Rejected`) is reconciled by later status queries, with idempotent retries and an `AgtIntegrationLog`.
4. **Two-level key management.** The software-producer key pair is generated locally, the private key never leaves the producer environment, the public key is registered in the Partner Portal. Taxpayer keys are obtained per company from the Taxpayer Portal and stored in `CompanyTaxProfile`. No private key is ever committed or shipped to the client.
5. **Legal numbering** is sequential and gap-free per `FiscalSeries`; finalised `FiscalDocument`s are **immutable** (corrections only via credit/debit notes).
6. **Official catalogues, never invented:** `TaxExemptionReason` and tax rates/regimes are loaded from official AGT annexes; exemptions store both the reason code and the legal ground.
7. **SAF-T (AO) is treated as a contract over the data model:** master-data and document entities must carry every field SAF-T requires; a `SaftExport` module produces XML validated against the official XSD, covered by conformance tests.
8. **Master Data is extended with fiscal fields** before the transactional modules (see ADR impact and the roadmap): `Company` (see [ADR-0005](ADR-0005.md)), `Customer`/`Supplier` NIF + fiscal address + fiscal country + final-consumer flag, `TaxCode` tax type/category/exemption-reason/legal-reference/effective-dates, `Currency` exchange rates.
9. **AGT certification is a cross-cutting acceptance gate**, not a sprint: the emission → hash → signature → SAF-T → submission chain must be functional and certifiable before go-to-market.

---

# 5. Rationale

SAF-T and certification are cross-cutting: they constrain the entire data model and every fiscal document. Doing them first and centrally is the only way to avoid a rewrite of Sales/Finance and to keep tax logic consistent. Separating the SAF-T hash from the AGT JWS reflects that they are genuinely different mechanisms with different lifecycles (offline/immutable vs online/async). The asynchronous outbox keeps invoicing resilient to AGT downtime. Loading catalogues from official annexes avoids illegal documents from invented codes.

---

# 6. Consequences

### Positive

- One authoritative, testable home for fiscal compliance; Sales/Finance are compliant by construction.
- Certification-ready architecture; resilience to AGT outages.
- Consistent tax handling and a SAF-T export driven directly by the model.

### Negative

- A foundation phase (ADRs, Company/tenant, master-data fiscal extensions, fiscal document core, SAF-T, e-invoicing) precedes the transactional modules, delaying visible business features.
- Increased domain complexity and key-management responsibility.

---

# 7. Implementation Guidelines

- Model `DocumentHash` (SAF-T chain) and `ElectronicInvoiceSubmission` (JWS) as distinct concerns.
- Introduce money/rounding value objects (2 decimals, `.` separator, no thousands separator) used consistently in totals, the hash string, and SAF-T.
- Submission is an outbox worker; finalisation only computes the hash and persists the document.
- Homologation vs production endpoints are configuration, never hard-coded.
- This ADR states the architecture; the exact legal parameters remain `[VALIDAR]` (see §9) and are not fixed here.

# 9. Items Pending Legal / AGT Validation

These remain `[VALIDAR]` and must be confirmed against official AGT sources before the corresponding implementation:

- Complete IVA rate list, mappings and special regimes (7% / 5% / 1%, simplified regime, hospitality, food/agricultural inputs, Cabinda).
- Official `TaxExemptionReason` catalogue (AGT annexes) and legal grounds.
- Full legal list of fiscal document types and their rules.
- Exact **SAF-T (AO) XSD** version and mandatory sections.
- Certification process, prerequisites, cost and timeline.
- Numbering rules (annual reset, cancellation representation).
- Withholding taxes and Imposto de Selo applicability and rates.
- Legal retention period; official rounding rules; NIF validation rules.

Technical facts already confirmed by the owner (AGT API endpoints, Basic Auth, JWS RS256, the SAF-T hash string logic) are treated as confirmed, pending only the exact XSD/spec version.
