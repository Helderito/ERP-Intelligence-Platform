# 1. Introduction

ERP Intelligence Platform is a **commercial ERP product** built for the **Angolan market**: a reliable, robust and user-friendly alternative to the enterprise systems currently used in Angola and the wider Portuguese-speaking market, inspired by the best practices of systems such as Primavera, SAP and Microsoft Dynamics. The intent is a **fiscally compliant, commercially viable and profitable** product — not a prototype or a demonstration.

Because it will be sold to Angolan organisations, statutory fiscal compliance is a first-class, non-negotiable requirement of the product, including certification of the invoicing software by Angola's tax authority (AGT), SAF-T (AO) reporting, and the local IVA / withholding-tax regimes. The precise regulatory scope is defined in the dedicated Angola Fiscal Compliance work (see the Product Backlog and the corresponding ADR).

The project is developed as a real commercial product, following professional software-engineering methodology and using Artificial Intelligence agents (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor) as force multipliers, without compromising architecture or code quality. As a **genuine secondary outcome**, it also serves as the owner's flagship portfolio, reflecting his transition into Software Engineering, AI and solution architecture — but the primary measure of success is a product that customers in Angola can adopt, trust and pay for.

---

# 2. Product Vision

To become a **trusted, profitable ERP for the Angolan market** — a modern, cloud-native, AI-native platform that lets Angolan small and medium-sized enterprises run their operations and **meet their statutory obligations with confidence**, at a lower total cost and with a better user experience than the incumbent alternatives.

The platform shall be:

- **Fiscally compliant** with Angolan legislation (AGT-certifiable invoicing, SAF-T (AO), IVA and withholding taxes, legal document numbering).
- **Reliable and robust** — production-grade quality, data integrity and auditability suitable for financial and fiscal records.
- **User friendly** — designed for real operators, in Portuguese, with a low learning curve.
- **AI-native and analytics-ready** — intelligent assistance and real-time business intelligence built in, not bolted on.
- **Commercially viable** — deployable and supportable at a sustainable cost, with a clear path to profitability.

As a secondary benefit, delivering the above also demonstrates the owner's competencies in Software Engineering, Software Architecture, Artificial Intelligence, Full-Stack Development, DevOps and Business Intelligence.

---

# 3. Mission

Build a modern ERP solution using industry best practices, using Artificial Intelligence tools as development accelerators, without compromising the quality of the architecture and the code.

# 5. Target Audience

The platform is designed first for **small and medium-sized enterprises in Angola** — retail, distribution, services and similar businesses that must issue compliant invoices, control stock and manage finances under Angolan law. The wider Portuguese-speaking market (e.g. other PALOP countries, Portugal) is a natural secondary expansion once the Angolan fiscal core is proven.

Buyers and users include business owners and managers, finance and accounting staff, and operators handling sales, purchasing and inventory. Their decisive purchase criteria are legal compliance, reliability, ease of use and total cost — the product is designed against those criteria, not against a feature checklist alone.

# 11. Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful when it is a product a real Angolan business can adopt and pay for:

**Commercial & compliance (primary):**

- The invoicing software is certified by the AGT and issues legally compliant documents.
- SAF-T (AO) reporting and the Angolan IVA / withholding-tax regimes are supported.
- The core operational modules (sales, purchasing, inventory, finance) work end to end for a real business.
- At least one real Angolan business can run its operations on the platform.
- The product is supportable and priced for a sustainable, profitable operation.

**Engineering (enabling):**

- The architecture is documented and the core modules are implemented.
- The system has automated tests and fully automated deployment.
- The platform has Artificial Intelligence integration and analytical dashboards.
- Data integrity and auditability meet the bar for statutory fiscal records.

**Secondary:**

- The work also stands as a professional portfolio of the owner's engineering, architecture and AI competencies.

# 12. Long-Term Vision

The long-term goal is for ERP Intelligence Platform to be an **established, profitable ERP in Angola** and, from that proven base, to expand across the Portuguese-speaking market — competing on compliance, reliability, usability, embedded intelligence and total cost of ownership.

The platform shall evolve continuously, keeping pace with software-engineering and Artificial Intelligence advances, and deepen its fiscal and regional coverage as it grows.

Building this also advances the owner's evolution as a Software Engineer, Data Specialist and AI professional — a real and valued outcome, but subordinate to building a product that succeeds in the market.
